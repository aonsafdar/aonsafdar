## Research

Medical images are a poor match for a vision transformer built around natural-image layout. My doctoral project customises the tokenizer, and asks whether global attention should run over space or over features.

Two neighbouring lines ask the same kind of question. With colleagues at UCD I study foundation-model transfer for ocean-colour harmful-algal-bloom detection, including which part of the channel interface actually carries the gain. At KAUST I work on integrated sensing and communication, using passive cell-side 5G uplink data, with an interest in foundation models and generalization. I also study language-model agents that carry an open-ended vision-architecture programme. An oral paper from that work is on the [IEEE ICA 2026](https://attend.ieee.org/ica-2026/) programme in Kumamoto on 16 December 2026.
